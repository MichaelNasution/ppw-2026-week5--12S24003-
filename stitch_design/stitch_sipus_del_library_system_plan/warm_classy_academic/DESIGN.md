## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines academic digital experiences through the lens of warm scholarly minimalism. Crafted specifically for an esteemed university library ecosystem, the aesthetic moves away from institutional stiffness and sterile cold blues, adopting an atmosphere reminiscent of naturally lit reading rooms, rich linen-bound tomes, and quiet architectural presence.

### Tone & Personality
- **Scholarly & Poised:** Intellectually serious and authoritative, without resorting to archaic or bureaucratic tropes.
- **Warm & Welcoming:** Tactile, calming, and human-centric—inviting deep concentration, prolonged research, and effortless digital discovery.
- **Contemporary Editorial:** Clean architectural spacing, deliberate typographic hierarchy, and refined materiality that feels balanced and premium.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for students, academic researchers, and faculty who spend extended hours interacting with library catalogs, thesis repositories, and reservation workflows. The interface reduces visual fatigue through low-strain parchment neutrals, tactile borders, and soft contrast, cultivating feelings of focus, serenity, and academic pride.

## Colors

The color palette grounds the institutional legacy of Del in natural, organic hues. The traditional vivid royal purple is reinterpreted as an espresso-infused deep aubergine-indigo, flanked by warm amber reminiscent of polished oak desks and sunlit paper.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#312052`):** Del Heritage Aubergine. A deep, ink-tinted violet-indigo providing dominant authority for navigation headers, active states, key titles, and primary buttons.
- **Secondary (`#B45309`):** Burnished Amber. A radiant caramel-gold accent deployed for key highlights, featured collections, active lending notifications, and callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#475E4E`):** Academic Sage. A grounded, botanical olive green reserved for success indicators, "Available in Stacks" statuses, and verified academic badges.
- **Neutral (`#78716C`):** Warm Stone. A warm, earth-derived gray scale maintaining legibility and soft contrast without the harshness of neutral carbon blacks.

### Foundation & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF7F2`):** A soft, warm linen parchment tint applied across the root viewport to eliminate pure white glare during extended reading sessions.
- **Card/Container Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white reserved for raised content cards, dialogs, and reading panes to ensure sharp typographic rendering.
- **Surface Borders (`#E8E2D8`):** Gentle, warm stone outlines that structure content cleanly without optical clutter.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes clarity, sleek geometry, and contemporary editorial breathing room. Plus Jakarta Sans delivers an open, legible structure optimized for complex catalog scanning, citation inspection, and long metadata listings.

### Typographic Rules
- **Proportional Tightness:** Display and headline sizes incorporate subtle negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.025em`) to retain crisp composure on larger titles.
- **Micro-labels & Badges:** Metadata tags (such as call numbers, ISBNs, and circulation statuses) employ `label-sm` or `label-md` with positive letter spacing (`+0.02em` to `+0.03em`) and medium/semibold weights to ensure distinct scannability at small sizes.
- **Reading Rhythm:** Paragraph body text is paired with generous line height (minimum 1.55x) to maintain a literary flow across abstracts and article summaries.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured upon an adaptive 12-column grid anchored by a maximum content container of `1280px` on desktop viewports. The spacing system establishes consistent vertical cadences inspired by book page margins.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Behavior
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Enables persistent catalog filtering sidebars (`3 columns`) alongside live search result feeds (`9 columns`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Filter panels collapse into a drawer overlay; book cards reformat into 2-column or 3-column matrices.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column grid, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Information switches to clean single-column stacks with edge-to-edge reading comfort and horizontal swipe carousels for recommended titles.

### Spacing Cadence
- Content groupings inside cards utilize `space-sm` and `space-md`.
- Structural separation between discrete functional regions (e.g., search bar to facet tabs) employs `space-lg` to `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through warm tonal layering and soft ambient depth, avoiding harsh dark shadows.

### Ambient Warm Shadows
Shadows incorporate an aubergine-tinted warm umber tone rather than pure charcoal:
- **Resting Layer (Cards, Catalogs):** `0px 1px 3px rgba(49, 32, 82, 0.04), 0px 4px 12px rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.03)`. Subtle, quiet separation over `#FAF7F2`.
- **Hover/Interactive Layer:** `0px 4px 8px rgba(49, 32, 82, 0.05), 0px 12px 24px rgba(49, 32, 82, 0.07)`. A graceful lift indicating tactile feedback.
- **Floating Modals & Flyouts:** `0px 16px 36px rgba(49, 32, 82, 0.12), 0px 4px 10px rgba(49, 32, 82, 0.04)`.

### Tonal Boundaries
Every elevated surface is bounded by a crisp `1px` stroke tinted to `#E8E2D8`. This low-contrast boundary provides tactile definition and academic polish under varied ambient display conditions.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a refined roundedness language that feels modern and approachable while retaining structured discipline:

- **Base Cards & Surfaces:** Standard radius of `0.75rem` to `1rem` (`rounded-lg`), creating soft architectural containers for books, thesis metadata, and loan histories.
- **Form Controls & Inputs:** Precision radius of `0.5rem` (`rounded`), providing clear interaction affordances for search fields, date pickers, and filter controls.
- **Pills & Status Indicators:** Full capsule curves (`9999px`) reserved for classification tags, circulation statuses ("Available", "Reserved", "Reference Only"), and compact filter chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Filled Del Heritage Aubergine (`#312052`) with pure white text, subtle warm focus ring (`#B45309`), and `0.5rem` radius. Used for "Borrow Book", "Reserve Seat", and primary search triggers.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface `#FFFFFF` with `#E8E2D8` border, text `#312052`, shifting to `#FAF7F2` on hover. Used for "View Details", "Download Citation", and filters.
- **Tertiary/Ghost:** Transparent background with warm amber (`#B45309`) text, providing a quiet action for inline links and subtle actions like "Clear All Filters".

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Main Catalog Search:** Extra-height (48px) field with `#FFFFFF` background, `1px` solid `#E8E2D8` border, and warm focus highlight (`#312052` border with `0 0 0 3px rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.15)`). Prefixed with a refined search glyph.
- **Form Inputs:** Soft `#FAF7F2` resting background switching to `#FFFFFF` on interaction, paired with `label-md` headings floating above in `#312052`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Fully rounded (`9999px`) with soft tinted background fills:
  - *Available:* `#475E4E` text over `rgba(71, 94, 78, 0.1)` background.
  - *On Loan:* `#B45309` text over `rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.1)` background.
  - *Archived / Del Special Collection:* `#312052` text over `rgba(49, 32, 82, 0.08)` background.
- **Interactive Filter Chips:** `#FFFFFF` background with `1px` border `#E8E2D8`, featuring an active state that turns `#312052` with white text.

### Cards & Book Displays
- **Catalog Card:** Clean `#FFFFFF` container with `1px` border (`#E8E2D8`) and resting warm shadow. Includes a structured thumbnail region for publication covers, title in `title-md`, author metadata in `body-sm` (`#78716C`), and an anchored bottom shelf featuring call number and loan status.
- **Reading Room / Workspace Cards:** Structured metric layouts displaying live seat availability, power outlet icons, and noise-level classifications.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Checkboxes:** `0.25rem` radius, bordered in `#78716C`, filling with `#312052` and a crisp white checkmark when checked.
- **Radio Buttons:** Concentric ring styling with `#312052` active indicator dot and warm caramel focus accents.

### Lists & Tables
- **Citation & Catalog Tables:** Alternating rows using `#FFFFFF` and subtle `#FAF7F2` tinting, separated by ultra-light `1px` dividers (`#F0EBE1`). Header rows feature uppercase `label-sm` in `#78716C` with generous horizontal padding.
